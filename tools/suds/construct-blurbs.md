# Construct definitions for /suds-measures

These one-line definitions appear on the site: in the tooltip when someone hovers
a construct blob, and under each construct heading in the list view.

**Nothing here publishes until you change `status: draft` to `status: yours`.**
`build_data.py` skips every draft entry, so an unreviewed definition simply does
not render — the site degrades to showing no blurb rather than showing mine.

The draft text below was written by Claude on 2026-08-19 and was briefly live.
None of it came from your CSV, `ui.R`, or `server.R` — it was invented. It is
kept here as a starting point to revise or discard, not as a suggestion to keep.
These are definitional claims about psychometric constructs published under your
name, so they should be yours.

Format: a `##` heading naming the construct exactly as it appears in the data,
then a `status:` line, then the definition on the following line(s).

---

## Use
status: draft
Quantity, frequency, and pattern of consumption.

## Severity
status: draft
How entrenched the disorder is — dependence and problem severity.

## Consequences
status: draft
Harms and negative outcomes that follow from use.

## Withdrawal
status: draft
Symptoms experienced on cessation or reduction.

## Urges
status: draft
Craving and momentary desire to use.

## Expectancies
status: draft
What a person believes the substance will do for them.

## Motives
status: draft
Reasons a person gives for using.

## Self-Efficacy
status: draft
Confidence in the ability to abstain or cut back.

## Recovery
status: draft
Progress, readiness, and change over the course of treatment.
